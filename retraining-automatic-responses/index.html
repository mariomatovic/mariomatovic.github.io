RE-TRAIN AUTOMATIC RESPONSES

This idea is intended to re-train automatic responses that our brain constantly runs, especially under pressure, in times of fear and uncertainty.

A huge amount of psychological self-improvement tries to control the mind through the thought layer. Essentially we are thinking about changing our thinking.

Someone wants to reduce anxiety, so they analyze the anxious thought. They challenge it. Replace it. Reframe it. Tell themselves they are safe. Think positively. Try to convince themselves to feel differently. (“I shouldn't be anxious.” “Why am I thinking this?” “I need to be confident.” “I shouldn't care what they think.” “I need to relax.” )

But the underlying system is still producing the state/action. It is like trying to control a car by standing next to it and telling the driver what to do, instead of actually driving the car. The important observation is that humans already have another kind of control that is much more direct. If I want to grab a glass, I don't consciously calculate how to move my shoulder, elbow, wrist and fingers. I don't verbally instruct myself. I simply intend to grab it — and the brain's automatic machinery executes everything necessary.

It has various applications but most importantly in times of uncertainty, pressure, fear -> issue a direct nonverbal command → RELAX (without words, look at the cards below) → allow the automatic system to execute it.

Other use cases are potentially:

  1. Clear mind of thoughts: choose a desired direction/state (NEUTRAL) by issuing a direct nonverbal command → NEUTRAL → allow the automatic system to execute it
  2. For complex commands → creating an image in the brain, and issuing command towards that complex state.

Four controls to test
01 / RELAX

RELAX

Every muscle releases unnecessary tension at the same time.

Briefly picture your entire body completely relaxed.

Then simply intend that state to happen.

COMMANDCompletely relaxed.
02 / NEUTRAL

NEUTRAL

When you reach for a glass, you don't think about how to do it. You simply intend to take it.

Use the same principle with your mind.

Simply intend: no thoughts.

Nothing to think about. Nothing to do. Just be.

03 / CONFIDENT

CONFIDENT

I trust my ability to handle what is in front of me.

The core state is certainty in your own ability, not positive thinking or convincing yourself.

Look at the representation. Don't think about confidence. Simply intend the state of trusting your ability.

COMMANDI can handle this.
04 / COURAGE

COURAGE

Fear can be present, but it does not control action.

Look at the representation. Don't think about courage. Simply intend the state of being completely committed to your goal.

COMMANDI’m going.
