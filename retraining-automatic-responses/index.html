A new way to train the automatic layer

Retraining
Automatic Responses

We usually try to change our mental state by thinking about it. This method explores something more direct: deliberately intending a state and allowing the brain's automatic machinery to execute it.

The idea

Our brain constantly runs automatic processes shaped by what we have learned — skills, experiences, expectations, emotions, habits and responses to situations.

Under pressure, fear or uncertainty, many responses happen automatically. We often try to control them by thinking: “Calm down.” “Relax.” “Be confident.” But thinking about a state is not the same as directly producing it.

When you reach for a glass, you don't think through your shoulder, elbow, wrist and fingers. You simply intend to take it — and the automatic machinery executes what is necessary.

The experiment

Can we use the same direct intention for mental and bodily states?
↓
Intend the state.
Allow the automatic system to execute it.
Four controls to test
01 / NEUTRAL

NEUTRAL

No thoughts, no mental processing, nothing to do.

Use the same principle as reaching for a glass. Don't fight thoughts. Don't replace them. Don't analyze.

COMMANDSimply be.
02 / RELAX

RELAX

Every muscle releases unnecessary tension at the same time.

Briefly picture your entire body completely relaxed. Then simply intend that state to happen.

COMMANDCompletely relaxed.
03 / CONFIDENT

CONFIDENT

I trust my ability to handle what is in front of me.

Don't think about confidence or convince yourself. Simply intend the state of trusting your ability.

COMMANDI can handle this.
04 / COURAGE

COURAGE

Fear can be present, but it does not control action.

Access complete commitment toward the intended goal despite fear. Don't think about courage. Simply intend it.

COMMANDI'm going.
